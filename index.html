About the lab

How the lab is meant to work.

This is the curation layer for product thinking. Ideas enter only when they solve a real problem, can be explained clearly, and can become a working artifact.

01

What goes in

Real problems, observed friction, and buildable concepts with a clear user.

02

What stays out

Random trends, AI slop, fake use cases, and anything without a clear journey.

03

How the journey works

Discover the experiment, inspect the story, filter by track, then open the case study.

Life beyond the lab

When an experiment grows, it can move into a fuller product story.

The lab is a controlled space for exploration. Serious work stays on the main portfolio, and the most interesting ideas can later become their own product or case study page.

Feedback loop

Use the lab to review, refine, and decide what deserves to be built next. Every state should remain visible: featured, launch, research, play, archive, or next.

AI operating model

How the lab's AI work will run.

Weekly cycles keep the output focused. AI helps with research, drafting, structure, and build prep. You keep the approval gate.

01

Cadence

Weekly by default. The goal is sustainable, reviewable output rather than noisy volume.
